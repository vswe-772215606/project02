## New findings, beyond the Money Map

- A bill print taking over ~5 s holds confirm's SQLite write lock; other writes then fail with P1008 → HTTP 500
  (a waiter's add failed after 5.2 s during an 8 s print). Ordinary polling does not trigger it: a busy-floor
  control (2 pollers every 100 ms + 20 confirms) passed twice.
- Machine-speed write bursts raise P1008 on the fire-and-forget `sessionRepo.touchLastUsed`
  (`middleware/requireAuth.ts:35`) and sometimes on the request itself. Low risk at human pace.
- Five wrong PINs from one waiter lock every waiter out (next correct PIN → 423) — also in the morning audit.
- The 13.08 migration seeded `costPrice` only for dona self-ingredients and recipe dishes; old UNTRACKED dishes
  went live with NULL, i.e. zero food cost, unless someone typed one since.

## Tried and rejected, gotchas

- `pkill -f serve-headless` inside `docker exec … bash -lc` killed its own shell (exit 143), twice. Use
  `pgrep -f "serve-headles[s]"` or a script file.
- Claude-in-Chrome clicks, element-ref clicks and typing never reached the renderer; page-script `.click()` and
  the native `value` setter plus an `input` event did. Login only worked because the fields were prefilled.
- PINs 1111 / 2222 are refused ("PIN is too easy"); fixtures use 5738 / 4926.
- Moving the fake clock past 8 h expires admin sessions (401) — harness bug, fixed with `world.relogin()`.
- Prisma `@default(now())` columns use the engine's real clock, not the faked JS `Date`; reports read JS-set
  timestamps so the fake clock works, and the diagnostic's backdating check relies on `createdAt` being real.
- One "control" (Qarzlar balances vs the reports' Qarz qoldig'i) was really a consequence of the repayment
  race; relabelled as issue 29 rather than reported as a harness failure.
